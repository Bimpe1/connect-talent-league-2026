## The whole script in one go
Welcome! Here's how the league works, from sign-up to earning points. Step one: enter your first name and last
initial. Step two: enter your email. No password: we email you a link. Step three: tick the privacy box. Only
facilitators can see your email. Step four: choose learner or volunteer. Step five: pick the team you'd like to join,
then tap Complete Registration. Finally, open the link we email you. A facilitator approves you, then you can start.
Every weekday, a short challenge appears: about five to fifteen minutes. Answer by typing, a voice note, or a photo.
Your facilitator reviews your effort and awards points for your team. Claim ten bonus points each week by attending in
person at the Connect Talent centre. Your facilitator confirms it. Check the leaderboard any time to see your team's
rank and individual scores. That's the whole workflow. Have fun, and enjoy learning!

## Recording tips
- **Warm, friendly and unhurried.** The lines are paced for about 150 words a minute, so there is no need to rush.
- **Record each line on its own**, then place it on the timeline at the start time above. Leave a little silence
  between lines. If a line finishes early, let the silence sit; don't speed up to fill the time.
- Start each line right on its time (the caption appears a fraction of a second later). Don't run over into the
  next line's slot.
- Say **"email"** and **"link"** clearly, and "No password" firmly: the old video said the opposite.
- British English, and the button is called **Complete Registration**.
- Export as MP4 (H.264). Any video editor works (CapCut, Clipchamp, iMovie, DaVinci Resolve). With ffmpeg:
  `ffmpeg -i connect-talent-league-walkthrough.mp4 -i voice.m4a -c:v copy -c:a aac -shortest with-voice.mp4`
